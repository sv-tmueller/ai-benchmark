Controls

  • Drag the background – turn the whole cube around.
  • Drag a sticker – twist a layer; release to snap.
  • Wheel / pinch – zoom.
  • U D L R F B – face clockwise, with Shift counter-clockwise. Keys follow the current view: F is the face towards you.
  • M E S – middle slices.
  • X Y Z – rotate the whole cube (not a move).
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y – undo / redo.
  • Scramble starts a game: timer and move counter start with your first turn and stop when the cube is solved.
