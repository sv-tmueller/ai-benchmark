HydraSense

Meet HydraSense, the water bottle that pays attention so you don’t have to. A built-in sensor quietly tracks every sip throughout your day, while a soft, ambient glow offers a gentle nudge when it’s time to drink. No alarms, no noise. And when you want the full picture, HydraSense syncs seamlessly with iOS and Android health apps.

Crafted from vacuum-insulated stainless steel, its 750ml body keeps water cold from your first meeting to your evening run. One USB-C charge delivers thirty days of effortless hydration tracking, about as low-maintenance as wellness gets.

Available in matte black, sage green, and coral, HydraSense fits your desk, your bag, and your pace. Because feeling your best shouldn’t be another task on your list. It should simply flow.
