HydraSense

Your day runs on focus. Focus runs on water. HydraSense keeps the two connected, quietly.

A built-in sensor tracks every sip from first coffee to last email, and when you've gone too long without one, the bottle answers with a soft glow. No buzzing, no nagging; just a gentle light at the edge of your attention. Your intake syncs with the health apps you already use on iOS and Android, so hydration sits alongside your sleep, steps and training.

The rest is simply a very good bottle: 750ml of vacuum-insulated stainless steel that holds water cold through back-to-back meetings and the commute home. One USB-C charge lasts 30 days, long enough to forget it needs charging at all.

Choose matte black, sage green or coral. Then get back to your day, a little better looked after.
