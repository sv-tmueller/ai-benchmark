# Meet HydraSense®

You optimize everything else—sleep, steps, screen time. So why does hydration keep slipping?

HydraSense notices when you don't. A built-in sensor quietly tracks each sip across your day, syncing effortlessly with your iOS or Android health apps, so hydration finally sits alongside the rest of your wellness data. Instead of buzzing at you from across the desk, it simply begins to glow—a soft, patient invitation that waits until you're ready.

The hardware holds its own: 750ml of vacuum-insulated stainless steel keeps water refreshingly cold through morning commutes, marathon meetings, and evening workouts. A single USB-C charge delivers thirty days of power, making HydraSense one less device to babysit.

Choose matte black for minimalist appeal, sage green for everyday calm, or coral for a brighter start. Your afternoon slump just met its match.
