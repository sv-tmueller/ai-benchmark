Doing this naively (binding the composer's own depth texture while still rendering into it) risks a feedback hazard on some drivers. A separate quarter-resolution depth-only target is essentially free on a mid-range GPU and gives clean occlusion semantics — `if (linFrag > linScene) soft = 0` for occluded, else `smoothstep(0, 3m, dz)` for the graze zone. **Why additive blending on the plume layers:** Fire radiates light, not reflects it. Additive accumulation of the core + sheath gives the overdriven blowout near the nozzle that bloom then pushes into a believable glare. The shock diamonds are a `pow(sin(...), 6)` axial modulation gated by `(1 - expansion)` so they vanish as the plume bell widens with altitude — physically motivated by underexpanded flow regimes. **Why the camera uses exponential smoothing with hard cuts:** Broadcast cameras don't lerp between setups — they cut. Within a setup, the operator has neuromuscular lag (~τ=350ms here) plus micro-shake. The `cutFlag` snaps instantly on phase change, then smoothing resumes. FOV changes are also smoothed so a zoom feels like a human operating a lens, not a tween. **Why ACES + bloom threshold 0.92:** ACES preserves highlight rolloff so the white vehicle holds detail while the plume core (multiplied by 4.5 in-shader) sails past 1.0 and gets picked off by bloom.
