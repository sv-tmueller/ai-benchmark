🚂 Weichensteller

Colored trains leave the tunnel one after another. Guide every train to the station of its own color by switching the junctions.

  • Click a junction to toggle it. The yellow stripe and the arrow show where it points.
  • Right station: +1 point. Wrong station: −1 life. You have three lives.
  • Trains run at slightly different speeds. Use the two passing loops to let a fast train overtake a slow one. If two trains collide, the game is over.
  • Over time the trains get faster and appear more often.
  • Drag the mouse to rotate the camera, use the wheel to zoom.
