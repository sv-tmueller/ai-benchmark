Weichensteller

You are the pointsman of a model railway.

  • Colored trains leave the tunnel one after another.
  • Click the switches so every train reaches the station of its own color.
  • Right station +1 point · wrong station −1 life (you have 3).
  • If two trains collide, the game is over.
  • Trains get faster and come more often — stay sharp!
