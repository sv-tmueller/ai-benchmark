WEICHENSTELLER

THE POINTSMAN
  • Colored trains leave the tunnel one after another. Guide each train to the station of its color.
  • Click a junction to switch it. The yellow arrow and glowing track show where it points.
  • The lamp above the tunnel shows the color of the next train.
  • Right station: +1 point. Wrong station: −1 life. You have 3 lives.
  • If two trains collide, the game is over. Express trains (white stripe) are faster!
  • Drag to rotate the camera, scroll to zoom.
