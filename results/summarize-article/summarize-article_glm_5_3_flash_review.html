Gradient descent is an iterative optimization algorithm that minimizes a model's loss function by adjusting its weights in the direction opposite to the computed gradient. Its variants—including SGD, mini-batch SGD, and Adam—trade off convergence speed versus stability, and proper learning-rate tuning is critical because rates that are too small converge slowly while those too large diverge. Modern frameworks like PyTorch and TensorFlow automate gradient computation using automatic differentiation, making gradient descent practical for deep networks with millions of parameters.
